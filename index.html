    Lighter shades above, Wada’s original colours in the middle, darker below

    Click a swatch to copy its hex, or a combination under the colours to open it. The middle row, marked 和田 三造, holds Wada’s original colours, never altered. The steppers above and below each colour set its own number of lighter and darker shades; Shades in the toolbar sets every colour at once. A swatch marked limit had no room for a full step: it stops at the lightness limit, and the steps after it repeat the same colour.
